Setup and troubleshooting for Local Dictation on iPhone.

Set up the keyboard

  1. Open Local Dictation. Download a speech model over a reliable connection and allow it to finish preparing.
  2. In iPhone Settings, open General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and choose Local Dictation.
  3. Open its keyboard settings and enable Allow Full Access. This lets the keyboard exchange commands and completed text with the app.
  4. Start a session in the app and allow microphone access. Return to your text field, switch keyboards with the globe key, then tap the microphone to dictate.

Start and end a session

Start in the app, or configure an app shortcut, Back Tap or the Control Center control. Background starts need microphone permission, an installed model and enabled Live Activities. Enter your iPhone passcode if prompted.

End the session from the app, keyboard or Live Activity. During a session, the microphone stays on between dictations and iOS shows its microphone indicator.

When something needs attention

The keyboard is missing
Check that it is enabled in Settings. Some apps and secure text fields restrict third-party keyboards.
Dictation is unavailable
Open the app and check its setup checklist, model status and microphone permission. Start a fresh session. Basic typing works without Full Access.
A model download stops
Check your connection and available storage, then retry in the app. Model preparation can take time.
Text is waiting for insertion
Check the destination field before using the keyboard’s manual insertion option. The app never presses Send for you.

Manage saved text

History stays inside the app. Choose its retention in Settings, delete entries, or turn History off to clear saved dictations. See the privacy policy for details.

Still need help?

Email LocalVoiceIos@ibro.me with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and the steps that caused the issue. Please leave out private audio, transcripts and sensitive screenshots.
