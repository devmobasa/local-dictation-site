Privacy policy

Your speech is processed on your iPhone.

Effective 25 October 2026

Audio and recognition

Local Dictation’s App Store version processes speech on your device. We do not receive your audio or transcripts. The app has no accounts, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

A listening session begins with your action. The microphone stays active between dictations, with a rolling half-second buffer in memory. iOS shows its microphone indicator. Ordinary dictation does not save audio files. End a session in the app, keyboard or Live Activity; session time limits and interruptions also end it.

Keyboard and local text

Full Access lets the keyboard exchange commands and dictation results with the app through a shared container on your iPhone. The keyboard does not record audio, make network requests, read app History or use the clipboard. Basic typing remains available without Full Access.

Completed text is inserted into the app and field you choose. That destination app handles inserted text under its own privacy practices. Local Dictation never presses Send automatically.

History and deletion

When History is enabled, successful dictations are stored in the app’s private container with their language, model, duration and time. Files use iOS data protection and are excluded from device backup. Default retention is seven days; you can change it, delete entries or turn History off to delete saved dictations.

The app also stores settings, speech models and temporary local files used to deliver text to the keyboard. Removing the app deletes its private container; you can delete retained History from the app first.

Model downloads

The app connects to Hugging Face and its download infrastructure to obtain speech models and tokenizer files. These providers receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and the requested file. Audio and transcripts are not sent with these requests. After a model is installed, dictation runs locally.

See Hugging Face’s privacy policy for its handling of download traffic.

This website and support email

This site uses GitHub Pages hosting and contains no analytics, cookies or forms added by us. GitHub may process connection information when serving the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you send, and use them to respond. Avoid sending private recordings or transcripts. You can request deletion of your support correspondence.

Questions and policy updates

Contact the Local Dictation developer at LocalVoiceIos@ibro.me about privacy or support. Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
